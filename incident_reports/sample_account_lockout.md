# SIMULATED TRAINING EXAMPLE: account lockout

Ticket: SIM-003. Fictional scenario; no directory or account actions were run.
Impact: one fictional user cannot sign in to a training application.
Priority: Medium for a single blocked user; suspicious activity would increase
urgency according to the organization's actual incident policy.

Simulated observations: sign-in reports locked access. No password, account ID,
MFA code, identity document or security log is collected in the training store.
Simulated investigation: record sanitized wording and onset, stop repeated
attempts, ask whether a recent password change or unexpected prompt occurred.
Possible causes: repeated attempts or stale saved credentials; neither is proven.
Action: refer the scenario to the authorized identity team, who would verify
identity and investigate under policy in a separately authorized environment.
Resolution: not established in this lab. Final status: In Progress.
Escalation: identity team; security team if unexpected activity is reported.
No unlock, password reset, policy bypass or account enumeration is performed.
Learning: recognize the difference between documenting symptoms and having the
privileges and verified evidence to perform account recovery.
