# Simulated incident reports

All reports here are fictional training examples, not executed diagnostics or
real incidents at FinacPlus or any employer. They correspond to SIM-001 through
SIM-003 in data/sample_tickets.json. Their observations and outcomes are invented
for learning and clearly marked as simulation. Actual execution evidence belongs
in docs/verification.md. No real identities, credentials or network details are
included. Escalation does not imply resolution.
